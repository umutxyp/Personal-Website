## The problem with word filters

Advertisement spam does not arrive as a clean `discord.gg/` link. It arrives spaced out,
punctuated with symbols, translated into a language your filter does not cover, or burned
into an image so no text exists to match at all.

Sylon reads the message the way a moderator would. The ad detection is a model, not a
regex, and it runs on the image content too.

## What it runs

- **Moderation suite** — ban, kick, mute, warn, clear, with cases, logs, and per-role
  limits.
- **AI advertisement protection** — invites, ads, and scams in any language, image content
  included.
- **Spam protection** against message floods, repeated content, and cross-channel image
  raids.
- **Ticket system** with HTML transcripts, office hours, satisfaction surveys, and case
  tracking.
- **Join gate** — screens every account at the door: how old it is, whether it has a
  picture, what it calls itself, and for bots, who added it.
- **Raid protection** that judges the joins together, plus a one-command **server
  lockdown** that pauses invites, raises verification and slows every channel, with a
  timer that lifts it again.
- **Harassment protection** for insults, threats and targeted harassment in any language —
  off by default, because it is the server's call.
- **URL scanner** that checks where a link actually goes, and **appeals** so a punished
  member has somewhere to say it was wrong.
- **Leveling** from message and voice XP, with reward roles, multipliers, and custom rank
  cards.
- **Giveaways** with entry requirements, bonus entries, and one-click winner rerolls.
- **Role menus**, **auto role**, **word filter**, **suggestion board**, **tags**, **scheduled
  announcements**, **counter channels**, **temporary voice channels**, and
  **welcome/goodbye** automation with custom images.

Every moderation action is written down, with the reason attached, in a channel the server
chooses — because a filter that deletes a real member's message and says nothing has done
damage nobody can find out about.

Everything is configurable from Discord or from the web dashboard, and the dashboard shows
live counters for threats blocked and moderation actions taken.

## Where it stands

**%sylon.servers% servers · %sylon.users% users · %sylon.uptime% uptime** — including the
communities around my own products. A moderation bot at its best is invisible, so it grows
by being found when an owner goes looking for a fix, not by being overheard.

## Older than it looks

Sylon is the oldest thing I run. It started in February 2020 as a Discord bot called
**Server Support** and kept that name for six years — a name that described a helpdesk, so
people assumed it was a ticket bot and nothing else. In April 2026 it became Sylon and got
its own site, keeping the same account and every server on it.

## Stack

Discord.js · Node.js · MongoDB · AI classification · Next.js dashboard · Nginx ·
self-hosted Linux.
