## The problem with word filters

Advertisement spam does not arrive as a clean `discord.gg/` link. It arrives spaced out,
punctuated with symbols, translated into a language your filter does not cover, or burned
into an image so no text exists to match at all.

Sylon reads the message the way a moderator would. The ad detection is a model, not a
regex, and it runs on the image content too.

## What it runs

- **Moderation suite** — ban, kick, mute, warn, clear, with cases, logs, and per-role
  limits.
- **AI advertisement protection** — invites, ads, and scams in any language, image content
  included.
- **Spam protection** against message floods, repeated content, and cross-channel image
  raids.
- **Ticket system** with HTML transcripts, office hours, satisfaction surveys, and case
  tracking.
- **Server guard** and anti-raid controls.
- **Leveling** from message and voice XP, with reward roles, multipliers, and custom rank
  cards.
- **Giveaways** with entry requirements, bonus entries, and one-click winner rerolls.
- **Role menus**, **auto role**, **word filter**, and **welcome/goodbye** automation with
  custom images.

Everything is configurable from Discord or from the web dashboard, and the dashboard shows
live counters for threats blocked and moderation actions taken.

## Where it stands

**500 servers · 300K users · 99.9% uptime** across all shards — including the communities
around my own products.

## Older than it looks

Sylon is the oldest thing I run. It started in February 2020 as a Discord bot called
**Server Support** and kept that name for six years. In April 2026 it
became Sylon and got its own site.

## Stack

Discord.js · Node.js · MongoDB · AI classification · Next.js dashboard · Nginx ·
self-hosted Linux.
