## The company is younger than most of its products

Codeshare Technology Ltd was incorporated in August 2025. Four of the six products it runs
are older than the company, and two of them are older by more than five years — the oldest
Discord account we own was registered in 2020. We did not start a company and then decide
what to build. The things already existed, and at some point it became obvious they needed
a company around them.

## What we actually are

A small studio that builds software for online communities and runs it. Six products, each
with its own domain and its own audience: two Discord bots, a Minecraft data platform, two
community platforms, and an AI chat. No agency work, no consulting, and nothing built for a
client.

- **[Beatra](https://beatra.app)** — Discord music bot in %beatra.servers% servers with
  %beatra.users% users.
- **[Sylon](https://sylon.app)** — AI Discord moderation in %sylon.servers% servers with
  %sylon.users% users.
- **[MCStat](https://mcstat.org)** — Minecraft data platform: %mcstat.servers% servers
  tracked, %mcstat.players% player records, an open API.
- **[JustDiscord](https://justdiscord.org)** — Discord server and bot directory with
  %justdiscord.emojis% free emojis and stickers.
- **[JustAnime](https://justanime.me)** — anime and manga tracker with %justanime.titles%
  titles.
- **[Dotrai](https://dotrai.com)** — AI chat that searches the live web, runs real code,
  and shows its reasoning.

Running them ourselves shapes everything else. Whoever can fix a product at two in the
morning is the person who wrote it, so a decision to change something is something we act
on this week. It also means we carry every consequence of building something badly.

## The core stays free

Beatra plays music. Sylon guards a server. MCStat gives its data away through an open API.
Dotrai answers questions. None of that is behind a payment, and none of it is a trial that
runs out.

That is a constraint, not a slogan. A product that cannot charge for the thing it exists to
do has to be genuinely cheap to run, and there is no second revenue line quietly propping
up a product nobody wants.

## When something is not working, we say so

The developer marketplace that ran on codeshare.me for years closed in September 2026. It
never found its audience and it was by a distance the most expensive thing we operated, so
we wrote down why and published it instead of letting the site go quietly dark.

Beatra is the other half of the same habit: shut down in 2022, dark for three years, and
that is at the top of its story rather than buried at the bottom.

## How we got here

- **February 2020** — the Discord bot that eventually becomes Sylon is built. It is called
  Server Support.
- **November 2020** — the bot that eventually becomes Beatra is built. It launches the next
  year as MusicMaker and is shut down in 2022.
- **August 2022** — codeshare.me is bought and runs a developer marketplace.
- **March 2025** — work on Dotrai starts.
- **August 2025** — Codeshare Technology Ltd is incorporated in England and Wales. Four of
  the six products already exist.
- **October 2025** — Beatra comes back after three years off, under a new name and on its
  original account.
- **December 2025** — MCStat launches.
- **April 2026** — Server Support becomes Sylon and gets its own site.
- **August 2026** — JustDiscord and JustAnime launch three days apart.
- **September 2026** — the marketplace closes; codeshare.me becomes the studio's own site.
