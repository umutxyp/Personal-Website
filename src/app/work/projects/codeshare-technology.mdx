## What Codeshare is

Codeshare Technology is the software studio I founded and run. It builds for online
communities — the servers, the trackers, and the tools people reach for every day — and
keeps the part that matters free. Every product is run by the people who wrote it, on
infrastructure we operate ourselves.

[codeshare.me](https://codeshare.me) is the company's home: who we are, what we build, and
where each product lives.

## The six products

- **[Beatra](https://beatra.app)** — Discord music bot: YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music,
  Deezer, SoundCloud, Tidal and more in a voice channel.
- **[Sylon](https://sylon.app)** — AI Discord moderation: ads, invites and scams caught in
  any language, plus tickets, server guard and logs.
- **[MCStat](https://mcstat.org)** — Minecraft server and player analytics: live counts,
  server listings, player profiles, leaderboards and a public API.
- **[JustDiscord](https://justdiscord.org)** — Discord server and bot directory with 91K+
  free emojis and stickers.
- **[JustAnime](https://justanime.me)** — anime and manga tracker: rate every episode,
  follow studios, talk it over with the community.
- **[Dotrai](https://dotrai.com)** — AI chat that searches the live web, runs real code,
  and shows its reasoning.

## How the products are built

- **The core stays free.** Premium tiers exist, but the thing a product is *for* never
  sits behind a payment. That forces every product to be cheap to run and genuinely worth
  using.
- **Self-hosted.** No product depends on a platform that can change its pricing under us —
  we run the servers, the databases, and the workers.
- **Built in the open where it helps.** SDKs, plugins, and starters ship as open source:
  the [JustDiscord SDK](https://github.com/justdiscordorg/sdk), the
  [MCStat plugin](https://github.com/mcstatorg/MCStat-Plugin), and Discord bot starters
  like [MusicBot](https://github.com/umutxyp/MusicBot).

## Where it started

Codeshare began in 2019 as a Discord community for developers. In August 2025 the studio
was incorporated as Codeshare Technology Ltd.
