## Dream. Optimize. Think. Reason.

Dotrai is the AI product in the Codeshare lineup: a free chat for brainstorming ideas,
writing code, studying a topic, or just having a conversation — right from the browser.

Most AI chats hand you a paragraph and ask you to trust it — and a fluent wrong answer
looks exactly like a fluent right one. Dotrai's position is that the answer is not the
product; the working is:

- **The reasoning is visible** while it happens — open the thinking panel on any answer and
  see the steps, not just the conclusion.
- **It searches the live web**, and answers cite the pages they came from, with the sources
  kept on the message.
- **It runs real code in a sandbox**, so what you see is what the code actually printed,
  not the model's guess at what it would print.

## Built for everyday use

- **Code help** — write, debug, and understand code across languages, with inline
  syntax-highlighted examples.
- **Streaming responses** — the answer appears word by word instead of after a long wait.
- **Saved conversations** — every chat is stored on your account, so you can pick it up
  again on any device.
- **Files and images** — it reads what you upload, not just what you type.
- **Incognito chats** — a visible mode, not a buried setting: nothing from an incognito
  conversation is written down.
- **Runs anywhere** — desktop, tablet, or phone, entirely in the browser. Nothing to
  download.

## Free, on purpose

Work on Dotrai started in March 2025, and after most of its life in development it opened
to everyone in 2026. Sign-up is an email and a password — no credit card, no setup, no
waitlist. Like every Codeshare product,
the core of Dotrai stays free — which means it has to be cheap to run and good enough to
keep using.
