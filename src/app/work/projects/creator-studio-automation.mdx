## Why

My audience sits across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and Discord, and the products I run do
not build themselves. Publishing consistently and shipping consistently only coexist if
the publishing part is mostly automated, so I built a content pipeline that takes an idea
to a scheduled post without a free weekend attached.

## The pipeline

1. **Ideas inbox.** A bot collects prompts out of Discord, X, and the questions users
   actually ask under my products, tagging each one by theme and priority.
2. **Script builder.** A dashboard pulls a prompt, injects real code from what I am
   currently building, and exports scene-by-scene.
3. **Distribution.** Scheduled jobs publish to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok,
   and send the email recap.

## What it changed

- Roughly 3x the publishing cadence with no editor added.
- Feedback loops run backwards: what people ask about in the comments decides which
  feature I build next in Beatra, MCStat, or JustDiscord.
- The audience and the products feed each other instead of competing for the same hours.
