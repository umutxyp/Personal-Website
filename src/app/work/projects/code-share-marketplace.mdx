## Where the company started

Codeshare began in 2019 as a Discord community for developers trading snippets. It is now
the marketplace that gives Codeshare Technology its name — and the parent brand behind
Beatra, MCStat, JustDiscord, and Sylon.

## What sells here

Game top-ups · gift cards · game and social accounts · software licenses · source code ·
templates and themes · UI/UX and design assets · APIs and integrations · automation and
bots · freelance digital services — across **30+ categories**.

## Buying without getting burned

Digital goods are easy to fake, so the trust layer is the product:

- **Escrow protection on every order.** Money moves to the seller after delivery, not
  before.
- **Verified sellers only.** Approval is manual, and it can be revoked.
- **Instant auto-delivery** for keys and licenses — no waiting on a human to paste a code.
- **YouTube subscriber gating**, which unlocks a listing only for people subscribed to a
  creator's channel, through the YouTube API.
- **Real-time chat** between buyer and seller, with dispute resolution behind it.

## For developers

The [Codeshare CLI](https://github.com/codeshareme/codeshare-cli) version-controls snippets
from the terminal, so a shared piece of code is something you push rather than something
you paste into a form and lose track of.

## Where it stands

- **10K+ users** with public profiles.
- **13.9K+ shared snippets**.
- **30+ categories** spanning code, licences, accounts, and services.
