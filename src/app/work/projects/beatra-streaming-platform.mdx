## Music that follows you across Discord

Beatra is a platform rather than just a bot: the same queue and the same account work
inside Discord, in the browser, on the desktop app, and inside a Discord Activity.

It currently runs in **%beatra.servers% Discord servers** for **%beatra.users% users**, and
it is free.

## It has been shut down before

The bot behind Beatra was first built in November 2020 and launched in 2021 as
**MusicMaker**. In 2022 I shut it down, and it sat dark for three years. In October 2025
it came back under a new name, on its original Discord account — and that history stays
at the top of the story, because a product history with the failure taken out is a
brochure.

MusicMaker was shut down after the summer of 2021, when YouTube switched off Groovy and
Rythm — the two biggest music bots on Discord, more than eight hundred million users
between them — five weeks apart. Beatra was rebuilt around every source instead of one.
And because the account was never deleted, every server that had added the bot years
earlier still had it when it came back; none of them had to invite it again.

## Nine sources, one queue

YouTube · YouTube Music · Spotify · Apple Music · Deezer · SoundCloud · Tidal · Audius ·
Qobuz. Paste a link from any of them, or search once and let smart autoplay keep the room
going.

## What people actually use

- **24/7 mode** — the bot stays in the channel whether or not anyone is listening.
- **Smart autoplay** across 20+ genres, so a queue never simply ends.
- **Audio filters** — bassboost, nightcore, 8D, and the rest, applied live.
- **Playlists** that survive the session, shared per user and per server.
- **Beatra Wrapped**, plus listening analytics, top-song and top-artist pages, and public
  profile and server pages.
- **Custom bots** — a tenant system that runs a community's own branded bot on the same
  infrastructure.
- **Premium** at $1.49/month for people who want to skip voting and lift the limits.

## Running it

Playback is the easy part; staying up is not. Beatra runs across sharded gateway
processes, a pool of voice workers, and companion services that keep source tokens fresh —
each one its own systemd unit so a bad deploy in one place does not silence 40,000 servers
at once. Uptime sits at 99%.

## Stack

Discord.js v14 · Node.js · Lavalink · Next.js dashboard · React · Electron · MongoDB ·
Redis · Nginx · self-hosted Linux.
