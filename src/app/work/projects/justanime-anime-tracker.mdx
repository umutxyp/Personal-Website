## Rate the episode, not just the show

A season score flattens everything: the episode that made the show and the recap episode
nobody needed end up behind the same number. JustAnime lets you rate and review anime,
manga, and **every single episode**, so a score can say *which part* was good.

## The catalogue

- **123K** anime and manga titles
- **176K** character records
- **1,190** airing schedule entries

## What you can do

- **Track everything you watch and read** — anime and manga in one list.
- **Rate and review** a series, a volume, or an individual episode.
- **Follow studios** and see what they release next.
- **Follow people** — see what they are into and find your next favourite through their
  reviews and scores.
- **Browse** trending titles, ratings, and a seasonal calendar, plus music videos and
  trailers.
- **Achievements and history** on every profile.

## JustAnime Sync

Keeping a list up to date by hand is the reason most people stop keeping one. The
**JustAnime Sync** extension for Chrome and Firefox (desktop and Android) watches for you:
once you are far enough into an episode or chapter, it marks it on your JustAnime account —
and on any AniList or MyAnimeList account you have linked there.

It works on **200+ streaming and reading sites**, from Netflix and Crunchyroll to MANGA Plus
and VIZ.
