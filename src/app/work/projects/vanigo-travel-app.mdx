## Travel like a local, wherever you go

The best travel tips come from people who live there: where to eat, what is worth seeing,
what a taxi should cost. Vanigo brings them into one app for iPhone and Android. Open it
in a new city and you see what is around you, what people who have been there thought of
it, what is on tonight and how to get there.

## Everything for the trip

- **A map of %vanigo.places% places** — cafés, sights, beaches and markets, searchable by
  name or by what you feel like, with walking and driving routes, voice guidance, public
  transport and offline maps.
- **Reviews** with stars, words and photos, and votes for the ones that helped.
- **Events and trips** — join or host an event, once or every week, and put the places you
  want to see on a day with your own notes.
- **Ask locals** — a question about taxi prices, food or where to pay, answered by people
  in the city you are in.
- **A travel toolkit** — a currency converter that also works offline, a guide to each
  country, the local emergency numbers, the transport apps people use there, and a card
  that shows your driver a place's name and address in the local language.
- **Chat** with the people you follow, end-to-end encrypted, with messages in other
  languages translated on your phone.

The app speaks **%vanigo.languages% languages**.

## Paying small shops by QR

In much of Southeast Asia and Latin America, small shops are paid by scanning a local QR
code that only works with a local bank app. Vanigo is built to pay those codes — VietQR,
PromptPay, QRIS, Pix and more — from USDT or USDC: you scan the shop's own code, see the
amount in local currency with the rate and the fee, and confirm; a licensed payment
partner pays the shop in its own currency.

The wallet is self-custody. Its key is made on your phone, and Vanigo cannot move your
money. QR payments are being switched on country by country, and the first one asks you to
verify your identity.

## Where it is
